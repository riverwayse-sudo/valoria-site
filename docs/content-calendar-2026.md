# Valoria Institute — Content Calendar & Editorial Operating Plan

## Objective

Build a consistent search, authority and demand engine around Valoria's core journey:

**Search / social / events → VALU Snapshot → account → professional profile → full VALU → marketplace → opportunity**

The editorial system exists to make professional capability understandable, searchable and actionable. Every major content asset should lead to one clear next action.

## Publishing cadence

Target: **8–12 useful articles per month**.

Minimum operating cadence:
- 2 Insight articles/week
- 1 deeper authority article/week
- 3–5 social derivatives per core article
- 1 event-led content sequence for each approved event
- 1 monthly employer/marketplace article
- 1 monthly VALU/PRIME explainer

Do not publish filler to hit volume. Each article must have a search intent, audience, internal-link destination and conversion purpose.

## SEO architecture

Every article should contain:

1. One primary search intent.
2. One primary keyword/topic.
3. 2–5 semantically related terms.
4. A clear H1.
5. Useful H2/H3 structure.
6. A concise search-oriented introduction.
7. Internal links to relevant Valoria pages.
8. One primary CTA.
9. Related Insight links.
10. Article structured data.
11. Canonical URL.
12. Updated publication metadata.
13. A useful meta description.
14. A social derivative.
15. An explicit audience/use case.

### Internal-link rules

Insights should systematically connect:

- VALU articles → /valu
- PRIME articles → /prime
- Development articles → /develop
- Employer articles → /marketplace
- Marketplace articles → relevant marketplace destination
- Event articles → /events
- Related Insights → other canonical /insights/[slug] pages

Avoid orphan articles.

## Content quality gate

No article ships unless it is:
- genuinely useful
- specific rather than generic
- consistent with Valoria terminology
- aligned with PRIME/VALU where relevant
- written for a defined professional problem
- internally linked
- discoverable through the sitemap
- assigned a canonical URL
- connected to a measurable CTA
- free from duplicated or thin content
