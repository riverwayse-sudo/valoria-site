# Valoria Institute — Institutional Positioning & Website Copy Standard

**Status:** Working copy standard for the website content review  
**Authority:** Valoria Institute Website Review (20 pages), Valoria Website Content Overhaul (6 pages), and the 10 November 2026 close-out addendum.

## 1. Institutional position

Valoria Institute is the institution that establishes and operates a structured professional capability framework and standards.

It assesses capability through the VALU Index, develops capability through PRIME-aligned experiences, and supports professional representation and opportunity discovery through its connected platform and pathways.

**Canonical short description**

> Valoria Institute establishes the standards and infrastructure through which professional capability is assessed, developed, represented and connected to opportunity.

**Core brand line:** Worth. Built.

**Core narrative:** Make professional capability clearer, development more intentional and opportunity more meaningfully connected.

Valoria must not be positioned primarily as a Nigerian, African or local talent marketplace. Its institutional proposition should be understandable and relevant across markets. Do not claim worldwide operations, international accreditation, global partnerships or geographic reach unless those claims are independently evidenced.

The legal operating entity may be identified accurately in legal notices and relevant corporate disclosures. Legal domicile, governing law, office location, data-protection obligations, event time zones and payment currency are operational facts; they must not be used as the institution's headline market positioning.

## 2. Message hierarchy

Keep this hierarchy consistent across the homepage, About, PRIME, VALU Index, Develop, ATB Connect, ATB Spotlight, Marketplace, events, email and social content:

1. **Valoria Institute** — the institution, standards and governance.
2. **PRIME** — the five-cluster capability framework and its 17 skills.
3. **VALU Index** — the assessment result expressed in points; it informs capability understanding and development. It is not itself a professional certification.
4. **Valoria Develop** — PRIME-aligned capability development.
5. **African Talent Bureau / ATB Connect / ATB Spotlight** — connected pathways for professional representation, talent discovery, speaker discovery and relevant opportunity access. The African Talent Bureau name is a legal/brand name, not a geographic limit on Valoria's proposition.

Do not present the marketplace as the whole institution. The platform is how the institution's framework and standards become useful to professionals and organisations.

## 3. Audience propositions

**Professionals**  
Understand your capabilities, identify meaningful development priorities, build a clearer professional profile and become discoverable for relevant opportunities where eligibility and visibility requirements are met.

**Employers and organisations**  
Explore professional capability through a more structured representation of skills, experience and assessment context. Do not imply guaranteed performance or hiring outcomes.

**Event organisers and programme buyers**  
Discover speakers and facilitators through declared expertise, relevant experience and available evidence. Do not describe anyone as certified unless the relevant certification has formally been awarded.

**Development buyers**  
Commission PRIME-aligned development designed around defined capability priorities. Programme scope, facilitator suitability, delivery format, measurement and commercial terms must be confirmed before they are presented as guaranteed.

## 4. Voice and copywriting rules

- Institutional, assured, precise, intelligent and warm.
- Write with clarity before abstraction. Translate institutional concepts into tangible user value.
- Prefer active verbs: assess, understand, develop, demonstrate, represent, discover, connect.
- Avoid inflated claims, vague superlatives and luxury language unsupported by institutional substance.
- Avoid making every paragraph sound like a manifesto. Explain what Valoria does, how it works and why it matters.
- Use one clear proposition per section and one primary call to action per user journey.
- Use consistent spelling and terminology throughout the site. Use international English consistently; do not mix US and UK spelling arbitrarily.
- Do not use “human capital institution” as public-facing shorthand.
- Avoid “Africa first”, “Nigeria first”, “Nigerian professionals”, “local talent marketplace” and similar geographic-first positioning in public marketing copy.

## 5. Canonical terminology and claim controls

- **PRIME:** Presence, Relationships, Intelligence, Mastery and Enterprise. The framework contains 17 canonical skills.
- **VALU Index:** results are points, not percentages. Never display a percentage sign for VALU results.
- **Taster and full assessment:** the taster is a separate entry experience, not part of or deducted from the full assessment. The full assessment is a distinct subsequent journey.
- Do not expose internal question counts, validity-check mechanics or progress labels such as “1 of 58” or “1 of 9”.
- The taster does not produce the official VALU Index or a merit designation.
- Assessment completion, a VALU Index result, listing eligibility, public visibility and professional certification are separate states.
- Do not use “certified” for a professional, speaker or facilitator unless the relevant certification has formally been awarded.
- Do not describe the VALU Index as independently validated or independently assessed until that validation is complete.
- Do not publish profile counts, active opportunities, buyer demand, partnerships, programme outcomes or credentials unless current evidence supports the exact claim and the relevant workflow works end to end.
- Do not promise guaranteed hiring, bookings, professional success or capability outcomes.

## 6. Geographic and legal language

Public positioning should lead with the institution's capability standard and the needs it serves, not the country where the operating entity is registered.

Keep accurate jurisdictional disclosures in the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, contracts, invoices and other contexts where legally relevant. Keep office address, legal entity name, applicable law and data-protection references accurate. Do not delete or disguise these facts in the name of international positioning.

Do not substitute a new unsupported claim such as “global institution” or “worldwide network”. International relevance is a positioning ambition; geographic reach must be evidenced.

## 7. Page-level consistency checklist

Before publishing or revising any page, confirm:

1. Does the page identify Valoria Institute as the institution, rather than defining it as only a marketplace?
2. Does it use the canonical PRIME and VALU terminology?
3. Is the audience and practical benefit immediately clear?
4. Does it distinguish assessment, eligibility, visibility and certification accurately?
5. Are every number, credential, outcome, partnership and availability claim verifiable?
6. Does the language avoid geographic-first positioning while preserving required legal disclosures?
7. Does the call to action match the actual working user journey?
8. Does the page sound like the same institution as the homepage, About page, email and other service pages?

## 8. Reference copy

**Homepage institutional introduction**

> Valoria Institute establishes a structured standard for understanding professional capability. Through the VALU Index, PRIME-based development and connected pathways to professional visibility and opportunity, it helps individuals and organisations make capability clearer and more actionable.

**About-page introduction**

> Valoria Institute establishes and operates a professional capability framework. We help professionals understand and develop their capabilities, make their merit more visible and connect with relevant opportunities.

**Footer description**

> The institution establishing the standards and infrastructure through which professional capability is assessed, developed, represented and connected to opportunity.

These are reference formulations, not mandatory repeated slogans. Adapt them to each page's audience and purpose without changing the underlying meaning.
