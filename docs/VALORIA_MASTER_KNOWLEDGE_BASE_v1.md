## 1. Brand and positioning
- **Brand:** Valoria Institute.
- **Canonical brand line:** **Worth, Built.** (comma, not full stop between words).
- **Positioning:** Africa's professional capability marketplace.
- **Core belief:** Talent is not the problem. Infrastructure is.
- **Operating promise:** Develop. Surface. Connect.
- Valoria develops professional capability, makes merit legible, and connects professionals to relevant opportunities.
- Avoid calling Valoria a “human capital institution” or describing the public-facing product as only an institution. Prefer “professional capability marketplace” where describing the marketplace.

## 2. PRIME framework — canonical 17 skills
PRIME is the five-cluster capability architecture. Cluster names, themes and skill names must not be invented, paraphrased as new sub-skills, or replaced by generic placeholders.

| Cluster | Canonical theme | Canonical skills |
|---|---|---|
| **P — Presence** | How you show up | Communication; Negotiation; Personal Brand & Executive Presence |
| **R — Relationships** | How you connect | Emotional Intelligence; Conflict Resolution; People Development; Stakeholder Management |
| **I — Intelligence** | How you think | Critical Thinking; Strategic Thinking; Business Acumen; AI Fluency |
| **M — Mastery** | How you deliver | Execution & Accountability; Resilience & Self-Leadership; Adaptability |
| **E — Enterprise** | How you create | Commercial Creativity; Influence Without Authority; Human-AI Collaboration |

**Count:** 17 skills total (3 + 4 + 4 + 3 + 3). AI Fluency and Human-AI Collaboration are distinctive future-ready skills and must be visible on the public PRIME page.

## 3. VALU Index assessment architecture
Use **VALU Index** as the assessment/index name. Do not refer to a standalone product named “VALU” where the intended meaning is the assessment.

There are two stages:
1. **VALU taster:** 9 questions, Intelligence cluster only. It is directional and does not produce the official VALU Index score.
2. **Full VALU Index assessment:** 54 questions across all five PRIME clusters. This is the authoritative assessment for the official VALU Index score and designation.

Do not publish “15-question snapshot”, “15-question VALU”, or “15 questions across PRIME”. Do not imply that account creation is required before a visitor can begin the taster. Keep taster and full-assessment data/scoring distinct.

The public journey should be described plainly: **Take the 9-question Intelligence taster → complete the full 54-question VALU Index → build/complete the professional profile → become discoverable when applicable eligibility and review requirements are met.**

## 4. Canonical score designations
Use the five canonical designations and never substitute a three-tier model:
- **Force to Align With:** 80–100
- **Emerging Force:** 65–79
- **Developing Professional:** 50–64
- **Building Foundations:** 35–49
- **At the Starting Point:** below 35

An illustrative score of **84** maps to **Force to Align With**. Designations are merit signals, not paid or subscription tiers.

## 5. Marketplace and profiles
- One professional account maps to one canonical professional profile.
- A profile may hold multiple capabilities, including Talent, Speaker and Facilitator; capabilities are not separate professional identities.
- Assessment completion, profile completeness, eligibility and actual listing are distinct states. Do not promise automatic public listing where admin review or eligibility still applies.
- The homepage registry must only display profiles meeting a minimum public quality standard: a valid professional title, a specific role description, readable plain-text content, and no generic placeholders such as “Model”, “Growth Dev”, or “Valoria Professional”. Do not delete genuine participant records to solve a display-quality problem; correct the data or suppress unqualified profiles from public promotion pending review.
- Use clear, user-understandable wording. Avoid unexplained phrases such as “governance gates”; name the actual eligibility/review requirement or omit the phrase.

## 6. Content and conversion rules
- Keep the existing three audience paths: professionals, employers, and event organisers.
- The About page is broadly aligned and should be preserved except for terminology/punctuation corrections.
- Use a clear distinction between directional taster insight and official full-assessment results.
- Do not overstate the score as a guarantee of employment, speaking engagements or listing.
- Canonical copy: “Worth, Built.” and “How you create” for Enterprise.
- Remove duplicate or contradictory question counts, designation labels and old positioning across body copy, metadata, CTAs, cards, and footers.
