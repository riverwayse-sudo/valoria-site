# Repository instructions

## Mandatory copy governance

Before changing any user-visible copy in the existing Valoria Institute website, read `docs/CURRENT_SITE_COPY_GOVERNANCE.md` and follow it. The authoritative source is *Valoria Institute Website review.pdf* (updated 4 November 2026), not the separate global redesign audit.

Every copy-changing PR must cite the exact audit section and PDF page, include before/after wording, and verify that the statement matches current product capability and approved evidence. If the audit is ambiguous or does not authorise a change, do not improvise: preserve the existing copy and request approval.

Do not mix current-site fixes with the separate redesign project. Technical changes that do not alter visible wording may proceed independently. No unsupported institutional, accreditation, validation, partnership, certification, opportunity, outcome or user-count claims.

See `docs/CURRENT_SITE_COPY_GOVERNANCE.md` for the full binding policy and copy-change register template.
